As a cricket fan, I often wonder: “What would I do if I were the captain?” Fans question bowling changes and field placements, but usually have no way to test their ideas or understand the trade-offs before knowing the outcome.
Captain’s Call turns passive watching into an interactive captaincy experience. Fans inspect a T20 match situation, choose an eligible bowler, move nine fielders, select a bowling intention, and explain their strategy. They lock their decision before revealing the next over, encouraging thoughtful choices rather than hindsight.
CaptainAI assesses the bowler, field, intention, and match context together, highlighting strengths, vulnerabilities, suggested adjustments, and missing information. A reproducible simulator explores illustrative outcomes, while a transparent game score rewards coherent planning and risk awareness. The current demo uses clearly labeled synthetic replay scenarios, with future potential to help aspiring players practise captaincy under pressure.
I used OpenAI Codex and its AI coding agents to implement, test, and debug the application. The app includes a server-side OpenAI-compatible LLM integration for tactical feedback. Without an API key, it provides explicitly labeled deterministic Demo analysis instead of claiming that AI generated the response.
