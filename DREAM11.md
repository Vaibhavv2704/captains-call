# Dream11 submission draft

Cricket fans watching T20 matches constantly ask: “Why that bowler?” or “Why is no one there?” Captain’s Call turns those reactions into playable captaincy decisions.

Fans inspect an illustrative match situation, choose an eligible bowler, move nine fielders on an interactive cricket ground, choose a bowling intention, and explain their plan. They must lock their call before revealing the scripted next over. Responsive desktop/mobile layouts, dark/light modes, legal-field checks, and local decision history make the experience ready to demo.

CaptainAI evaluates the bowler, field, intention, and match context together. A server-side OpenAI-compatible LLM adapter supports structured tactical feedback; without credentials, clearly labeled deterministic Demo analysis keeps the product usable. Feedback identifies strengths, vulnerabilities, field conflicts, adjustments, and missing information without inventing player tendencies. A reproducible illustrative simulator shows baseline next-over possibilities, without claiming field-driven or calibrated win probabilities.

Codex AI coding agents helped implement and verify the application. The captaincy score is a transparent game metric, and the real-world lesson is clear: a good decision can still produce a bad outcome.
